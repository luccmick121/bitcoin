Bitcoin (BTC)

BEP-20 on BNB Smart Chain · fixed supply · verified contract

Bitcoin (BTC) is a BEP-20 representation of Bitcoin issued on BNB Smart Chain, the EVM-compatible chain of the Binance ecosystem. It is designed for users who need Bitcoin-denominated exposure inside BNB Chain applications — swaps, liquidity pools, and integrations — without leaving the network. The contract enforces a fixed supply of 21,000,000 tokens with 18 decimals, matching Bitcoin's unit convention, and is fully verified on BscScan.
